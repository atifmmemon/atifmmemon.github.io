“Agile Quality Assurance Techniques for GUI-Based Applications” by Qing Xie and Atif M. Memon. In Agile Software Development Quality Assurance, (Ioannis Stamelos and Panagiotis Sfetsos, eds.), 2007.

Abstract

This chapter proposes a process-based approach for assuring quality while developing in agile mode. The authors propose a new concentric loop-based technique, which effectively utilizes resources during iterative development. It is based on three types of testing, namely crash testing, smoke testing, and comprehensive testing. The overall approach is illustrated on the development of graphical user interfaces. The GUI model used to implement the concentric-loop technique is given in detail.
